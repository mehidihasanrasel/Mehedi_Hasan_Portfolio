About The Doctor

Meet Dr. Mehidi Hasan

MBBS (Dhaka) | MD (Cardiology) | FCPS (Medicine)

Dr. Mehidi Hasan is a highly distinguished Cardiologist and Internal Medicine Specialist with over 15 years of clinical experience. He completed his MBBS from Dhaka Medical College and pursued advanced training in Cardiology both at home and abroad.

Known for his patient-first philosophy, Dr. Hasan has treated thousands of cardiac patients, performed complex interventional procedures, and contributed significantly to cardiovascular research in Bangladesh.
